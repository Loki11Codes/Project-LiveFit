# Product Requirements Document (PRD) - Project LiveFit

## 1. Project Overview
LiveFit is an AI-powered nutrition and fitness tracking application designed to simplify the process of logging food, workouts, sleep, and body measurements through natural language processing.

## 2. Target Audience
Fitness enthusiasts, athletes, and individuals looking for an easy, friction-less way to monitor their health and fitness goals without manual database searching.

## 3. Key Features
- **AI Chat Interface**: Natural language logging for food, workouts, sleep, and weight.
- **Nutrition Tracking**: Automated extraction of protein, calories, carbs, fats, and fiber from text or images.
- **Workout Logging**: Volume tracking, PR monitoring, and exercise focus logging.
- **Sleep & Body Tracking**: Duration, bedtime, wake time, and comprehensive body measurement history.
- **Goal Management**: Configurable targets for different day types (Rest, Training, Lite).
- **History & Visualization**: Tabular and graphical representation of progress over time.
- **Theme Support**: Premium look with Light and Dark modes.

## 4. User Experience (UX) Goals
- **Minimal Friction**: Speak or type naturally to log data.
- **Fast Response**: AI should parse and confirm logs instantly.
- **Premium Design**: Modern, clean, and responsive interface inspired by Apple Health and other high-end fitness apps.

## 5. Success Metrics
- Accuracy of AI parsing for common food items and workout descriptions.
- User retention through ease of logging.
- System performance (latency < 2s for AI responses).
